# Vrinda-Store-Analysis
Analyzed Vrinda Store’s 2022 sales data using Microsoft Excel. Built an interactive dashboard with Pivot Tables, Charts, and Slicers to track sales, orders, customer demographics, and channel performance. Generated insights on top states, age groups, and sales channels, and provided data-driven recommendations to improve business growth.

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Vrinda Store 2022 sales data** using Microsoft Excel.
The objective is to identify sales trends, customer behavior, and key business insights to improve overall sales performance.

An interactive Excel dashboard was created to track KPIs such as:

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Gender-based purchasing behavior
* State-wise contribution
* Channel performance
* Order status distribution
* Age group contribution

---

 🎯 Business Questions Solved

1. Compare Sales and Orders using a single chart
2. Identify the month with highest sales and orders
3. Who purchased more in 2022 – Men or Women?
4. What are the different order statuses in 2022?
5. Top 5 states contributing to sales
6. Relationship between age group and gender
7. Which sales channel contributes maximum revenue?
8. Highest selling category

---

 📈 Key Insights

* Women contribute approximately **65%** of total purchases.
* Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the **top 3 revenue-generating states (~35%)**.
* Adult age group (30–49 years) contributes nearly **50% of total sales**.
* Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra contribute around **80% of total sales**.
* Most orders are successfully delivered (~92%).

---

 💡 Final Business Recommendation

To increase sales, Vrinda Store should:

* Target **Women customers aged 30–49 years**
* Focus marketing campaigns in **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh**
* Promote offers and coupons on **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra**
